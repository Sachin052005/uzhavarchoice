Irrigation System & Services

Our advanced irrigation systems are designed to optimize water usage and improve crop productivity. We provide end-to-end solutions including drip and sprinkler systems, fertigation units, automation, installation, and after-sales maintenance with full government subsidy assistance.

Mulching & Protected Cultivation

High-quality mulching sheets and protected cultivation solutions help conserve soil moisture, control weeds, and enhance crop yield. We offer supply, laying services, and technical guidance for shade nets, polyhouses, and crop-specific protected farming practices.

Fertilizer Advisory

Scientific fertilizer recommendations tailored to each crop and growth stage ensure balanced nutrition and higher yields. Our advisory covers macro and micronutrients, fertigation schedules, soil health improvement, and organic or bio-fertilizer solutions.

Pest & Disease Management

Effective pest, disease, and weed management solutions using IPM practices help protect crops sustainably. We provide crop-specific pesticide, fungicide, and herbicide recommendations along with eco-friendly bio-pesticide alternatives.

Seed Selection & Planning

Choosing the right seed is critical for successful farming. We guide farmers in selecting certified and hybrid seeds, planning seasonal crops, improving yields, and adopting crop rotation and intercropping techniques for long-term soil health.

Soil & Crop Diagnostics

Soil and crop diagnostic services help identify nutrient deficiencies and soil problems early. Our services include soil sample analysis, interpretation, fertilizer correction plans, and recommendations for managing problem soils effectively.

Landscape Design

Professional landscape design services for homes, commercial spaces, and institutions. We specialize in garden planning, green area development, aesthetic layouts, and sustainable landscaping solutions that enhance outdoor spaces.

Plant Supply & Garden Setup

We supply a wide range of ornamental, flowering, indoor, and outdoor plants along with complete garden setup services. Our solutions include avenue plantations, shade trees, lawn development, and decorative garden installations.

Garden Irrigation Systems

Efficient garden irrigation systems including drip lines, sprinklers, and automatic timers ensure uniform watering and water conservation for all landscapes.

Garden Maintenance

Regular garden maintenance services keep landscapes healthy and visually appealing with professional care and seasonal contracts.

Farmer Advisory

Expert farmer advisory services provide on-field consultation, crop diagnostics, and technical guidance with government scheme support.

Agri Input Supply

We supply high-quality fertilizers, pesticides, seeds, and irrigation materials trusted by farmers and institutions.
