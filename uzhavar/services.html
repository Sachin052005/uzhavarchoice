Our Services

We provide quality agricultural solutions to help farmers and businesses thrive. Explore our services below.

Irrigation System & Services

Our advanced irrigation systems are designed to optimize water usage and improve crop productivity. We provide end-to-end solutions including drip and sprinkler systems, fertigation units, automation, installation, and after-sales maintenance with full government subsidy assistance.

Mulching & Protected Cultivation

High-quality mulching sheets and protected cultivation solutions help conserve soil moisture, control weeds, and enhance crop yield. We offer supply, laying services, and technical guidance for shade nets, polyhouses, and crop-specific practices.

Fertilizer Advisory

Scientific fertilizer recommendations tailored to each crop and growth stage ensure balanced nutrition and higher yields. Our advisory covers macro and micronutrients, fertigation schedules, and organic bio-fertilizer solutions.

Pest & Disease Management

Effective pest, disease, and weed management solutions using IPM practices help protect crops sustainably. We provide crop-specific pesticide, fungicide, and herbicide recommendations along with eco-friendly alternatives.

Seed Selection & Planning

Choosing the right seed is critical. We guide farmers in selecting certified and hybrid seeds, planning seasonal crops, improving yields, and adopting crop rotation techniques for long-term soil health.

Soil & Crop Diagnostics

Soil and crop diagnostic services help identify nutrient deficiencies early. Our services include soil sample analysis, interpretation, fertilizer correction plans, and recommendations for managing problem soils.

Landscape Design

Professional landscape design services for homes and commercial spaces. We specialize in garden planning, green area development, aesthetic layouts, and sustainable landscaping solutions.

Plant Supply & Garden Setup

We supply a wide range of ornamental, flowering, indoor, and outdoor plants along with complete setup services. Our solutions include avenue plantations, shade trees, and lawn development.

Garden Irrigation Systems

Efficient garden irrigation systems including drip lines, sprinklers, and automatic timers ensure uniform watering and water conservation for all residential and commercial landscapes.

Garden Maintenance

Regular garden maintenance services keep landscapes healthy and visually appealing with professional care, pruning, fertilization, and seasonal maintenance contracts.

Farmer Advisory

Expert farmer advisory services provide on-field consultation, crop diagnostics, and technical guidance with extensive support for various government schemes and subsidies.

Agri Input Supply

We supply high-quality fertilizers, pesticides, seeds, and irrigation materials trusted by farmers and institutions for reliable and productive agricultural output.
